Problem 3. In the blocking flow problem (which arises in the blocking flow al­
gorithm for the maximum flow problem), we are given a directed acyclic graph
G = (V,E), 2 vertices s and t and capacities on the edges. The goal is to find a
flow f such that for every path P in E from s to t at least one of the edges of P is
saturated (i.e. f(v,w) = u(v,w)).
1. Is the following argument correct?
A blocking flow f is maximum since, if we take S to be the set of
vertices reachable from s in (V,E) by non-saturated edges, we get
¯
a cut (S : S) whose value equals the blocking flow, and hence the
blocking flow must be optimal.
If the argument is fallacious, show a blocking flow which is not maximum.
2. Show how to find a blocking flow in a graph G = (V,E) with n vertices and m
edges in O(m log n) time. (Your solution can be quite short.)
(FYI, Dinitz showed that one can solve a maximum flow problem in a general directed
graph by solving at most n blocking flow problems in directed acyclic graphs.)
