Question 9 1 / 1 pts
What are some of the most common types of private-sector computer crime?
Your Answer:
According to Nelson et al. (2024), common types of private-sector computer crime include data
breaches, intellectual property theft, unauthorized access to confidential information, embezzlement,
email harassment, gender and age discrimination, and insider threats where employees misuse
access to company systems for personal gain.
Reference:
Nelson, B., Phillips, A., Steuart, C., & Wilson, R. (2024). Guide to Computer Forensics and
Investigations (7th ed.). Cengage.
Question 10 1 / 1 pts
Give a brief explanation of a computer forensics lab auditing process.
Your Answer:
Nelson et al. (2024) describe the computer forensics lab auditing process as a systematic review to
ensure compliance with industry standards and legal guidelines. The audit examines evidence
handling protocols, security measures to protect evidence integrity, and operational procedures to
maintain accuracy and reliability in forensic analysis. This process also evaluates staff qualifications
and ensures that lab practices align with best practices in forensic investigations.
Reference:
Nelson, B., Phillips, A., Steuart, C., & Wilson, R. (2024). Guide to Computer Forensics and
Investigations (7th ed.). Cengage.
