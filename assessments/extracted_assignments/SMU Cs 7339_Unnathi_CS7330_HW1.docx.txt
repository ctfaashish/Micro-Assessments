Candidate key of Owner table based on Relationship between Owners and Teams.
i. team_name only:
If team_name is the only candidate key this would infer that every pair of team names
indexes a row in the table. In this example, owners have a 1:1 relationship with teams which
means an owner can only own one team and a team can be owned by one owner only as well.
ii. ownerssn only:
If ownerssn is the sole candidate key then each owner's SSN identifies a tuple. In this
scenario, the category between Owners and Teams is one-to-many (1:N) as an owner can
have many teams he owns whereas a team belongs to only a single owner.
iii. team_name or ownerssn:
If just one of team_name or ownerssn can be a candidate key, then each column is able to
uniquely identify a record in the table. Here, the owners and teams are still in 1 to 1
relationship (1:1) i.e., Each team will be owned by one owner and each owner will own only
a single Team.
iv. team_name and ownerssn as a combination:
A candidate key is a combination of columns in a table that accurately identifies each record
in the table. In this e.g., the relationship between owners and teams is many-to-many (M:N)
-- each owner can own multiple teams and each team can have many owners as well.
