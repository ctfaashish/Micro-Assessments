12.Describe how does Cyclic Redundancy Check (CRC) work? Criticize CRC. (10 points)
Both the sender and the receiver agree on a polynomial function, then you divide the block
message by the generator and append the remainder to the message. CRC is not a hash function and
therefore does not provide a sufficiently strong data integrity guarantee for packets.

20.Please briefly describe audit IDS and inline IDS (10 points)
In-line is faster but only provides a “local” view unless a lot of data is forwarded in real-time to
a central location. Audit is deeper but requires keeping lots of data.
21.Please briefly describe Host-based IDS and network-based IDS (10 points)
Quality and density of information is very high on Host-based however, capture is system
specific and performance is unreliable, and hosts are often target of attack.
Network based collect data from the network of a hub/switch and try to determine what is
happening from the contents of the network traffic. Can derive information that host based
logs can not provide.
