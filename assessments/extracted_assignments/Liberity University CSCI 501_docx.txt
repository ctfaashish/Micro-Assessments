3. In your Lab Report file, note the ways in which a security policy for a small
company might differ from one for a large organization.
In large organizations there may be a global policy that includes the least
common denominator of security mandates. All the sub-organizations may have
their own policies which are more tailored to that specific audience. In a smaller
company you wouldn’t have the sub-organizations and you would only have the main
policies that would be well-defined.
7. In your Lab Report file, document the six key characteristics of sound policy
and procedure.
1. Policy and procedure must be purposeful to fill defined needs and
serve an actual purpose.
2. Policy and procedure must be relevant and aligned with actual needs
and matched to the intended purpose.
3. Policy and procedure must be fully useable, actionable and capable
of implementation and enforcement.
4. Policy and procedure must be flexible for adaptation to reasonable
variations and exceptions.
5. Policy and procedure must be credible and fully justified and
enforceable in a consistent manner.
6. Policy and procedure must be developed and implemented with end-
user input and buy-in.
8. In your Lab Report file, describe a possible consequence of a policy failing
to exhibit each of those six characteristics.

Lab Assessment Questions & Answers
1. What makes a policy enforceable?
When it is supported and encouraged by senior management.
2. What are some ways in which the security policy for a small organization might
differ from one for a much larger organization?
In large organizations there may be a global policy that includes the least common
denominator of security mandates. All the sub-organizations may have their own
policies which are more tailored to that specific audience. In a smaller company you
wouldn’t have the sub-organizations and you would only have the main policies that
would be well-defined.
3. What are at least three of the six key traits of sound security policy?
1. Policy and procedure must be purposeful to fill defined needs and serve
an actual purpose.
2. Policy and procedure must be relevant and aligned with actual needs
and matched to the intended purpose.
3. Policy and procedure must be fully useable, actionable and capable of
implementation and enforcement.
4. Policy and procedure must be flexible for adaptation to reasonable
variations and exceptions.
5. Policy and procedure must be credible and fully justified and
enforceable in a consistent manner.
6. Policy and procedure must be developed and implemented with end-user
input and buy-in.
4. True or False: Because of the size of your fictitious company, it would be best for
your security policy to include supplemental documents or sub-policies.
5. Why is it important to back up System State?
It eliminates you having to reconfigure Windows back to its original state before
the system failure occurred.
6. Name at least three of the security procedures performed in Part 3 of this lab.
firewall, antivirus, , backup
7. Because there was no direct Internet connection in the virtual lab, how were the
antivirus software’s virus signature definition files updated?
They were updated from the ones we copied.
