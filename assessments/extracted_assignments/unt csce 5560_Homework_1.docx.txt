3. (10 points) What is DNS and what role does it play in the Internet and E-Commerce? On
October 21, 2016, Dyn, Inc., an Internet infrastructure company that provides DNS and
other services, sustained a distributed denial of service (DDoS) attack that took down a
significant portion of the Internet on the U.S. East Coast. What would be the effect if all
DNS servers were taken offline by a DDoS attack?
DNS refers to Domain Name System which acts as the internet’s phone book. It
makes the users of client computers to use names rather than numeric IP addresses
to identify remote hosts. In general, client computer sends the name of remote host
to a DNS server, which responds with corresponding IP address. DNS plays a key
role in providing the requests to specific pages. If the servers are taken offline, then
any major service provider would go blind and no services are offered to any clients.
4. (10 points) What is the difference between non-persistent and persistent HTTP?
 Persistent HTTP requires only single connection to send and receive multiple
HTTP responses or requests.
 Non-persistent HTTP must create a new connection for every request or
response.

5. (20 points) Internet growth has boomed without disruption because of
a. the client/server computing model. State some of the properties of a client and server
in this model and then contrast this model with peer-to-peer communication to show
why P2P would not work.
The client-server model is a distributed communication framework of network
processes among service requestors, clients and service providers. Clients
include Web browsers, chat applications, and email software, among others.
Servers include Web, database, application, chat and email, etc. The client-
server model differs from a peer-to-peer (P2P) model where communicating
systems are the client or server, each with equal status and responsibilities. The
P2P model is decentralized networking. The client-server model is centralized
networking.
b. the hourglass, layered architecture. In your own words, what does this mean and why
is it important? Your answer should be specific, stating one or two clear reasons for
this.
In the natural world, species that share the same ecosystem often compete for
resources, resulting in the extinction of weaker competitors. A new computer
model that describes the evolution of the Internet’s architecture suggests
something similar has happened among the layers of protocols that have
survived and become extinct on the worldwide network.
Understanding this evolutionary process may help computer scientists as
they develop protocols to help the Internet accommodate new uses and protect it
from a wide range of threats. But the model suggests that unless the new Internet
avoids such competition, it will evolve an hourglass shape much like today’s
Internet.
6. (10 points) As a key Internet technology, describe the importance of packet switching to
the Internet, which has in turn benefited the growth of e-commerce. As a hint, you may
want to compare packet switching to circuit switching and describe why packet switching
is more beneficial to support the growth of e-commerce.
The circuit switching system of network where the devices establish a dedicated
communication line to send a stream of data where as the packet switching avoids
the problem by allowing each packet to find its own path to destination, with no
dedicated connection required. This advantage of packet switching has reflected in
the reduction of cost and increased the network efficiency which benefited in the
growth of e-commerce.

network bandwidth that is filled, or wasted, with this header overhead. Note that your
answer may be in the form of an expression.
 You have 4 layers of protocol, and at each layer s bytes are added, so the total
number of header bytes is 4s.
 Your payload is M bytes, so the total packet size is M + 4s.
