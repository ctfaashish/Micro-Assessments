Chapter 1 – Introduction
1. Distinguish between vulnerability, threat, and control.
Vulnerability is a weakness in the system. Threat is a condition that could cause harm to a
system and Control is to prevent any threat from exercising vulnerabilities. For example,
a personal computer that is not password protected provides an easy access to anyone.
This is a vulnerability of the PC that could be used against us. Threat is someone
accessing the personal files in the PC and using it to benefit them and harm us. Control in
this case would be to create a strong password for the PC so that unauthorized person
would not have access to our personal files.
2. List at least three kinds of harm a company could experience from electronic espionage or
unauthorized viewing of confidential company materials.
Unauthorized viewing of confidential company material could reveal company’s trade
secret. This revelation could be used by their competitor to their benefit and harm
company’s reputation, sales and such. Company’s database could provide important
information about its customer and that could be misused or modified by an unauthorized
person for their benefit. This could harm bond and trust that had formed between the
company and its customer.

8. One control against accidental software deletion is to save all old versions of a
program. Of course, this control is prohibitively expensive in terms of cost of storage. Suggest a
less costly control against accidental software deletion. Is your control effective against all
possible causes of software deletion? If not, what threats does it not cover?
Alternative control against accidental software deletion that is cost effective is configuration
management. It is cost effective because software development control can prevent from causes
of software deletion. Configuration management allows replacement of the older version of the
software with a newer version while maintaining the integrity and performance. Replacement
software is compatible and works as well as the older version. They are well documented and
verified. Thus, they are the better alternative to saving old version of a program.
12. Do you think attempting to break in to (that is, obtain access to or use of) a computing
system without authorization should be illegal? Why or why not?
Attempting to break in to a computing system without authorization should be illegal because it
can expose user’s vulnerability and this could be used against them. Unauthorized person could
modify the data of user’s computing system and also deny them access to their own system.
Thus for these reasons unauthorized use of computing system should be illegal.
