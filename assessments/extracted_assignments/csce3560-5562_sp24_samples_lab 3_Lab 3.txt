In this lab, we will be running some covert-channel attacks and experiments.
Step 1 (10 pts):
Create two VMs on the same computer; you can do it by cloning your existing VM if your hypervisor
allows it (most probably it has this option). Show both your VMs from your hypervisor.
Step 2 (15 pts):
In this step, on VM1 you will be creating a heavy disk-write operation for approximately 10 seconds. For
this purpose, you will be using dd command with bs and count parameters as well as dsync info (as a hint)
and you need to find the right configurations for your computer.
You should automate the time measuring as well; i.e., there is one command that shows how much time it
takes to run an application (real-time, this is another hint for you).
You will be running at least 5 times and show them all, they should be relatively close to each other.
Which commands and which parameters did you use? Show your command, your parameters, and all the
times you have observed as a table and also as a single screenshot.
Step 3 (20 pts):
Now, we will use both VMs for the following purpose: We will try to understand if we are sharing the same
harddrive. Of course, we will simplify and will try to fine-tune for the moment. Now you need to run dd
command on VM2 with a change in the configurations: use ten times higher for the count size. Meanwhile,
you will be running the original dd command and check the time on VM1. Now you should observe that
the time you spent is higher at least 10%. This part will change based on your computer; therefore, you may
need to run different configurations and try to find the right one. You can end the command on VM2 now.
You will be running the same experiment at least 5 times and show them all, they should be relatively close
to each other.
What is the right dd command and configurations you found? Show your command, your parameters, and
all the times you have observed as a table and also as a single screenshot How much of time difference did
you observe in average?
