2. Vulnerability Brainstorm:
• Smart TV: Usually we don’t upload very often the software of the TV and that could lead
to threats and susceptible malware, also unsecured app downloads could be a problem.
• Play Station: Due to the online gaming, especially children could be good victims of
social engineering. In addition, players can get hit by DDoS attacks.
• PC: More common risk are viruses coming from shady downloads. Also old software.
• MacBook: Same as PC but also is vulnerable to public networks, as we spoke in class.
And if the Mac is lost or stolen our data could be compromised.
• Alexa: It can listen to every conversation you are having.
• iPad: Not keeping software updated. Also as MacBooks it can be stolen.
• iPhone: Same as iPad.
• Security cameras: Can be hacked and have unauthorized.
• Google Chromecast: Because you can share your screen and other personal
information, guest can maybe access and connect without being on the same wifi or
connect to your own wifi if you don’t have strong password.
• Wi-Fi router: Unauthorized access if they guess your password.

3. Mitigation Strategies
• Smart TV: Regularly update OS and apps, and avoid downloading from untrusted
sources.
• Play Station: Talk with kids about the dangers of talking and trusting things to people
they don’t know and the consequences.
• PC: Use antivirus, keep OS and programs updated. Be careful with the things you
download.
• MacBook: Use antivirus, keep OS and programs updated. Be careful with the things you
download. Don’t lose it and always keep an eye on it so they don’t steal it.
• Alexa: Configurate it the best way you can to improve the privacy. Disable the
microphone when not using it.
• iPad: Keep OS and apps up to date, use app permissions judiciously. Don’t lose it and
always keep an eye on it so they don’t steal it.
• iPhone: Keep OS and apps up to date, use app permissions judiciously. Don’t lose it and
always keep an eye on it so they don’t steal it.
• Wi-Fi router: Change default passwords and put a strong one.
• Google Chromecast: Don’t let anybody connect, keep a regular track on the google app
to see what devices are connected. Strong Wi-Fi password.
• Security Cameras: Strong passwords and updated firmware.
