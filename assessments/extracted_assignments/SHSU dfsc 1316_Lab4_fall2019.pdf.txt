DFSC 1316 Lab 4, Fall 2019
Mobile Forensics (200 points)
Submission Due: October 23, Wednesday
This lab is set for team work. Two students can make a group. Each group is
requested to make slides and present your work in class on Wednesday
October 23.
Please have your mobile devices such as smartphones. You may have text
messages, facebook, whatsapp, or other social account and conversation
records on your mobile devices. You may also delete a part of messages or
photos.
Search on the Internet and get suitable mobile forensics tools for your
devices and perform a forensics analysis about your mobile devices, and
submit a report about your work with screen shots. The report should include:
1. Your mobile devices (bands and models, operating systems, major
applications) (5 points)
2. Mobile forensics tools used and the reason to select the tools;
3. Each step and operation to perform logical acquisition and data analysis.
What types of data can be retrieved (including but not limited to call
history, text history, contact, browsing history, geo-tag information, etc)?
(100 points)
4. Can you recover the removed data? If yes, what kinds of data may be
recovered? If no, why? (45 points)
5. What have you learned from this exercise? (20 points)
The grading includes the report (170 points) and in-class presentation (30
points).
