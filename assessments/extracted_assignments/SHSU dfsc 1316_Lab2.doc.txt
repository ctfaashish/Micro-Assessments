DFSC 1316 Lab 2 ― Metadata
Submission due: Wednesday September 19, 2018@11:59PM
Where to submit: Blackboard
This lab has three parts, a total of 100 points. The first part covers the usage of PhotoMe, Exif
Pilot and/or EXIF viewer and editor tools to extract Meta data and modify Meta data of images.
The second part is about the extraction of metadata by using metadata analyzer. The third part
enforces you to obtain an open source pdf metadata tool by yourself and retrieve the metadata by
using the tool.
Part 1. PowerEXIF, Exif Pilot and/or PhotoMe (60 points)
This practice involve the usage of PhotoMe, Exif Pilot, EXIF viewer and/or JPEGSnoop
to extract Meta data and modify Meta data of images. You may not limit to the above
toolkits. If you are using Mac OS or Unix/Linux Operating System, please search on the
Internet and obtain relevant software/toolkits for your computer operating systems. You
may refer to the following webpage on the toolkits:
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_metadata_editors
Please submit your experimental results blackboard.
Step 1. To complete this practice, first, obtain photo metadata viewer and editor. You may
have a choice on PowerEXIF, PhotoMe, Exif Pilot or JPEGSnoop for this experiment.
1) Go to http://www.opanda.com/en/index.html, download Opanda PowerExif, and
install the software to your computer, or
2) Go to http://www.photome.de/, download PhotoMe, and install the software to your
computer.
3) Go to http://www.colorpilot.com/exif.html, download Exif Pilot, and install the
software to your computer.
4) JPEGSNOOP is available at http://www.impulseadventure.com/photo/jpeg-
snoop.html
Step 2. Next, go to the SHSU CS department webpage,
http://cs.shsu.edu/about/people.html, download two professors/instructors’ photos. You
may be also allowed to test other photos on your interest.
Retrieve the metadata of the photos and provide screenshots on the metadata and the
answers on the creation date, last modification date, digital camera used, and the encoder
(20 pts).
