Question 39 Not yet graded / 3 pts
For each of the following questions, provide an example of an
association rule from the market basket domain that
satisfies the following conditions. Also, describe whether such
rules are subjectively interesting or not.
a) A rule that has reasonably high support but low confidence.
b) A rule that has low support and high confidence.
Your Answer:
a) Below is an example for the rule that has reasonably high support but
low confidence.
{bread} -> {butter}
This suggests that customers who purchase bread are likely to also
purchase butter. The rule might have a high support because both items,
bread and butter, are commonly purchased, but the confidence might be
low because not all customers who buy bread also buy butter. This rule
may not be subjectively interesting because the association between
bread and butter is well-known and may not provide any new insights.
b) An example of an association rule that has low support and high
confidence could be:
{Lobster} -> {Champagne}
This rule suggests that customers who purchase lobster are likely to also
purchase champagne. The rule might have low support because lobster
and champagne are luxury items that not all customers purchase.
However, the confidence could be high because customers who do
purchase lobster are more likely to also purchase champagne. This rule
may be subjectively interesting because it suggests a pairing between two
luxury items that may not be immediately apparent to all customers.
