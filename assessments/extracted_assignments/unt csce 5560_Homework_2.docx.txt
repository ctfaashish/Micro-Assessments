1. (15 points) With regards to hardware scalability, identify and briefly describe the three
steps that can be taken to meet increasing demands for web site services.
The steps which are involved for increasing the demands of web site services are as
follows:
 Scalability of hardware Horizontally
It involves the addition of processing servers to the site and balancing the load among
them. It can be done using various PCs, so that if one machine fails another machine
would come into existence.
 Scalability of hardware Vertically
It includes the upgrading the servers such as converting from single core processor to
multi core processors. Among which some servers may be powerful, and some may be
little less when compared with others.
 Improvising the processing architecture
It can be achieved by recognizing the similar workloads and using the same kind servers
being allocated to them for the processing of data.

sufficient to provide security at the lowest layer (i.e., the physical layer or network access
layer, respectively) to prevent attacks?
 In case of OSI model, fragmentation attacks may occur, when the hacker

6. (15 points) Identify the three key points of vulnerability in an e-commerce environment.
Briefly identify/describe the at least two ways each vulnerability can be exploited and the
countermeasures that may be implemented to prevent these attacks.
Three key points of vulnerability in an e-commerce environment are:
 Client
A client may be attacked through computer viruses or the loss of the machine. Attack can
be of just leaving the system when the account is logged in. Counter measures can be
given as the personal firewalls, education about the attacks and importance of account
credentials, may also include the anti-virus software.
 Server
The server can be attacked by the malicious code, insider jobs, line tap, or the rogue
programs. For the server attacks the countermeasures may include firewalls, password
policy, anti-virus software and the encryption.
 Communication Channel
The communication channel can be exploited by the man-in-the-middle attacks, denial-of-
service, traffic analysis etc. Counter measures for securing the communication channel is
through encryption of communication channel by SSL, VPN’s or S-HTTP.
7. (10 points) Why are spyware and adware considered to be a security threat?
 Spyware can be used to track the usage of computer by different persons. It also
collects the personal information such as frequently visited websites, even credit
card details. When the spyware is installed, the user cannot find it. It can be in the
form of cookies.
 Adware may slow your computer and flood your screen with pop-up
advertisements. This program may collect information regarding the websites you
visit for generating targeted advertisements.
